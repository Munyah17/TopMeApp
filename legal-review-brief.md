# Brief for legal review — TopMe Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

**What TopMe is:** a Zimbabwe-based digital wallet app (topme.co.zw) — customers fund a wallet balance and spend it on airtime, mobile data, ZESA electricity tokens, bill payments (DStv, ZOL, TelOne), gift cards, wallet-to-wallet transfers, cash withdrawals, and insurance products.

**Pages for review:** `/terms` and `/privacy` (live on topme.co.zw).

**Specific points needing sign-off, in priority order:**

1. **Insurance agency language (Terms §2, Privacy "What we collect").** TopMe sells insurance products underwritten by Motions Microinsurance (and future partners) as their agent/dealer, not as an insurer itself, under their licence. Please confirm the wording correctly limits TopMe's liability and matches the actual agency agreement with Motions — this is the single most important clause to get right.
2. **Refund policy (Terms §4).** States money is auto-refunded to wallet on confirmed fulfilment failure, and that change-of-mind refunds aren't offered once a service has delivered. Confirm this is enforceable and matches consumer-protection expectations under Zimbabwean law.
3. **Data sharing with third parties (Privacy "Who we share it with").** Lists the payment processors (Paynow, EcoCash, Stripe, VitalPay) and, for insurance only, the underwriting partner. Confirm this satisfies whatever data-protection obligations apply.
4. **Account suspension / fraud language (Terms §5).** Confirm the suspension clause is broad enough to be useful but not overreaching.
5. **Age requirement (18+)** and **governing law (Zimbabwe)** — standard boilerplate, quick check only.

**Not covered yet, worth asking your lawyer whether it's needed:** a KYC/AML statement (if required for a Zimbabwean payment-adjacent business at TopMe's transaction volume), and a formal complaints/dispute-resolution procedure beyond the current support-ticket system.
